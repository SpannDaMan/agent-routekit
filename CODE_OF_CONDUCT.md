# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, impersonation, and disclosure of private data are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue when the report contains personal, private, or security-sensitive information.

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.
